# Tesora marketing deck — rubric and standing review

Scored August 2026 against the current build (14 pages, cream).

## The rubric

Six dimensions, each scored 1 to 5. The first three decide whether a page works.
The last three decide whether it survives a sceptical reader.

| # | Dimension | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Action title** | The headline states the takeaway, not the topic. Reading only the titles, top to bottom, tells the whole story. |
| 2 | **One idea** | The page argues a single thing. No second framework, no competing list. |
| 3 | **Real exhibit** | A screenshot, table, or diagram carries the argument. Prose supports it rather than replacing it. |
| 4 | **Restraint** | Rules rather than boxes. Accent colour spent once. Two type sizes per block. No status-colour scoring. |
| 5 | **Voice** | Thought progression, no em dashes, no staccato fragments, no laundry lists, nothing that reads as generated. |
| 6 | **Claim integrity** | Every number is defensible, attributed, and consistent with the numbers on other pages. |
