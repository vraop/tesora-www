The Harness

The Workbench is the surface. The Tesora Harness is the framework beneath it that turns a fast answer into one you can put your name on. Four agents do the work, and not one of them gets the last word.

Ingestion AgentReads

Takes loss runs, SOVs, dec pages, and rate manuals in whatever format they arrive, and normalizes them underneath the workflow you already run.

Insight AgentModels

Fits the curves, trends, and on-levels, then hands back a write-up the reviewer can check line by line.

Rating AgentDeploys

Turns the actuary's selections into a tested rater and the state exhibits that go with it, ready to file.

Audit AgentProves

Traces every value to a page, a row, a cell, and writes down anything it skipped, so a capped limit lands in the memo where a reviewer will see it.

Author, reviewer, and approver stay three separate people, enforced. Every value cites its source, the log is append-only, and the actuary owns every decision.

Tesora Case Study

The capacity problem

Where the actuarial hours went

✗

Raters versioned by filename

The rating logic lived in spreadsheets versioned by filename, which is to say not versioned at all. Changing one meant an IT ticket and a wait.

✗

One team, every priority at once

Pricing, competitive work, reserving, and rate filing all drew on the same handful of credentialed actuaries. Whatever was due soonest won.

✗

Filings built by hand, a state at a time

Each rate filing meant weeks of assembling exhibits and reformatting them to the next state's template.

✗

No quick read on the market

Comparing the carrier's rates to competitor SERFF filings happened rarely, and when it did it took days.

The real cost

None of this was a headcount problem, it was a throughput problem. The hours went to reconciling data and reformatting exhibits. The selections that actually move a loss ratio came last, when they came at all.

The carrier was capacity-bound, not market-bound. The demand for pricing work was there. The hours to do it were not.

~150 hrs/mo
Recurring actuarial workflow before Tesora, most of it spent moving and reconciling data before any analysis began.
